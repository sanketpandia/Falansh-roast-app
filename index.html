Falansh Loss Tracker

A living museum of scorelines so wide that even the FIFA radar thought it was looking at a weather map.

Legend says Falansh once picked up the controller with confidence, chose a formation with the seriousness of a Champions League final, and then proceeded to concede goals at a rate normally reserved for cricket scoreboards.

Back then, the margins were not losses. They were public infrastructure projects: long, expensive, and visible from space. Friends came for a casual match and left having witnessed a full documentary on defensive collapse.

Years passed. Consoles improved. Graphics became realistic. Controllers got haptic feedback. Falansh, however, remained beautifully consistent. The same heroic optimism before kickoff. The same suspicious silence after 4-0. The same post-match analysis involving lag, scripting, the sofa angle, planetary alignment, and one very unlucky pass in the 12th minute.

This tracker exists to preserve that heritage. Every score added here is another brick in the monument. Every huge margin is proof that tradition is alive, well, and still unable to defend a through ball.
